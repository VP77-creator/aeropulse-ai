End-to-End System Architecture (Phases 1 through 8)

Technical blueprint and methodology of the Flight Delay Analytics & Prediction System

1

Phase 1: Data Validation & Quality Audit

Audited 7,079,081 raw records across 12 monthly CSVs. Validated schema uniformity, handled cancelled/diverted flight partitioning, audited null patterns in cause-specific delay fields, and established data cleaning guardrails.

7.07M RecordsZero Schema DriftIntegrity Verified
2

Phase 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Analyzed multi-dimensional delay distributions. Discovered that Late Aircraft (40.44%) and Carrier Delays (34.51%) dominate delay minutes. Quantified the 3x delay acceleration between 06:00 AM (9.58%) and 18:00 PM (29.39%).

Seasonality AnalysisHourly RampCarrier Benchmarking
3 & 4

Phase 3 & 4: ETL Pipeline & Kimball Star Schema Data Warehouse

Designed a Kimball Star Schema with 1 Fact table (fact_flights) and 5 Dimension tables (dim_date, dim_carrier, dim_airport, dim_time_block, dim_route). Engineered columnar vector ETL with DuckDB achieving 0 referential integrity orphans.

Star SchemaDuckDB OLAPZero Foreign Key Orphans
5

Phase 5: SQL & OLAP Multidimensional Analytics

Implemented multidimensional analytical queries including Carrier-Month rollups, State/Airport hierarchical drill-downs, Route Pareto cumulative distributions, and surface congestion indexes.

Rollup & CubeWindow RankingRoute Pareto

Phase 6: Data Mining (Apriori Rules & K-Means Clustering)

Extracted association rules discovering high-lift delay conditions (e.g. CLT + Evening + Summer ⇒ 51.7% delay, 2.48x lift). Segmented 101 major airports into 4 behavioral clusters using unsupervised K-Means.

Phase 7: Machine Learning Prediction Pipeline

Trained a class-balanced HistGradientBoostingClassifier on 639K sampled flights with prospective temporal holdout validation (Months 11-12). Integrated Bayes smoothed target priors and threshold tuning (F1 max at 0.337).
